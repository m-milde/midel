You are writing a knowledge pack for a small neural network that learns one lesson at a time. It can hold about 120 words of context. A long essay will be cut off and will not be learned.

Write facts for this field only:
FIELD: Gemstones

Cover the well-known gem stones, not only the famous four. Include precious and common stones, a few organic gems such as pearl and amber, and a few facts about color, hardness, or origin when you are sure. One stone can appear more than once only when the second fact says something different.

Produce 40 facts. Reply with JSON only, no markdown fence and no commentary. Use this shape:

{
  "dataset_name": "gemstones",
  "facts": [
    {
      "id": 1,
      "category": "Gemstones",
      "fact": "One or two plain sentences.",
      "question": "A question a person would ask?",
      "answer": "The same fact, written as the direct answer."
    }
  ]
}

Rules for every item:
- The fact is 12 to 35 words. One main claim. A second claim is allowed only when joined with "but" or "though".
- Name the stone in both the question and the fact, so "What is ruby?" is not answered by a sentence that never says ruby.
- The question can be answered from that fact alone. Use what, why, where, when, who, or is/are.
- The answer is the same sentence as the fact. Do not answer with only "yes" or "no".
- Plain words. Write numbers as digits. Hardness may use the Mohs scale when you are sure of the number.
- No LaTeX, no citations, no URLs, no bullet lists, no quotes from books or articles.
- Skip a fact if you are not sure of the number, the color cause, or the place it is found. Do not invent a precise figure.
- Do not repeat these lessons, which are already known: gold does not tarnish or rust, and diamond and graphite are both pure carbon with different crystal lattices. You may still write a separate gemstone fact about diamond as a cut stone, as long as it is not that same sentence.

After the JSON, stop.
